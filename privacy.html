What this demo does

This sample website runs in your browser. The booking and contact forms show a confirmation on screen; they do not send, store, or share the information you enter. The newsletter form is also a front-end demo.

Images and fonts

Photos load from Unsplash and fonts may load from Google Fonts, so those services may receive a request from your browser when the page opens.

For a real travel business

Before launch, explain what information you collect, why you need it, how long you keep it, and how someone can contact you about their data. Have the finished policy reviewed for the places where you do business.
