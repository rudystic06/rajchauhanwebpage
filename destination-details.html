ITALY · A PLACE TO TAKE YOUR TIME

The Italian Riviera

Colourful little villages, impossibly blue water, and the pleasure of having nowhere urgent to be.

A coast made for wandering

We take the little train between villages, pause for focaccia while it’s still warm, and leave the afternoons open for a swim or a nap. Our local host knows the quieter paths and the family-run places that make this coast feel like itself.

A taste of the days

Meet us in Genoa, then follow the coast through Camogli, Santa Margherita, and the colourful lanes of the Cinque Terre. There’s a boat ride, a market morning, and a very good pesto lunch. We’ll make the practical bits easy and let the days find their rhythm.
